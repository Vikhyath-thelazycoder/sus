## T-1.7 platform check: does LangGraph fit the free plan?

**Bundle size (free limit: 3 MB compressed):** `wrangler deploy --dry-run` → **2,155 KiB raw / 502 KiB gzip (16% of the limit)**. ✅ Fits. Worker startup time: 22 ms.

**CPU time (free limit: 10 ms per request):** measured with `wrangler tail --format json` on the live Worker.

| Route | Samples | min | p50 | p90 | max | > 10 ms |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `/api/health` (Hono only) | 5 | 0 | 0 | — | 9 | 0/5 |
| `/api/admin/diag/graph` (2-node LangGraph, no LLM) | 40 | 4 | **14** | **82** | 133 | **24/40** |

All 40 graph requests returned 200: Cloudflare didn't reject them today, but 60% exceeded the documented 10 ms limit, and this was a **trivial 2-node graph**. The real pipeline has 7 nodes, so overhead will grow.

**Conclusion:** LangGraph **fits on size but not on CPU** on the Workers free plan. Hono itself costs ~0 ms, so the overhead is LangGraph's. Per the plan, the owner chooses the demo path:

1. **Workers Paid ($5/month)**: keep LangGraph; CPU limit becomes 30 s by default. Same platform as production; no code change.
2. **Free plan without LangGraph for the demo**: the same nodes run as plain function calls (≈0 ms overhead); LangGraph comes back in the MVP on the paid plan.
3. **Move the demo to Render**: owner's earlier fallback; requires the platform adapter for D1/Vectorize/Workers AI over REST, and Render's free tier sleeps when idle.
